About

Nine years of finding out what's actually happening, then fixing it.

I'm a Senior SEO, Performance Marketing and Analytics Specialist based in Ahmedabad, currently working with the Neat Everyday brand under Mangalam Groups. Over the past 9 years I've worked across e-commerce, SaaS and service businesses — including remote engagements for clients in the UK, USA and UAE — with the same underlying job every time: find out why a business isn't converting or ranking the way it should, and fix the actual cause.

That usually means starting with measurement before strategy. A lot of "SEO problems" and "conversion problems" turn out to be tracking problems — GA4 events that were never firing correctly, attribution that doesn't reflect the real customer journey, a Shopify store reporting numbers nobody trusts. I've spent a meaningful chunk of my career fixing that layer first — implementing GA4 e-commerce tracking and server-side Google Tag Manager — because every optimization decision downstream depends on it being right.

From there, the work is what you'd expect: technical SEO audits, keyword and content strategy, Shopify store and product page optimization, Google Ads and Meta Ads management, and CRO testing — all judged against the same question, did the number that mattered actually move.

I also use AI tools — ChatGPT and Claude — as part of daily work: researching faster, drafting and debugging code for internal dashboards and automations, and speeding up first-pass content or analysis. I don't treat AI output as final. Research gets checked against Search Console or SEMrush data, code gets tested before it ships, and any AI-assisted content goes through a factual review pass. It's a way of working faster, not a way of skipping the parts of the job that require judgment.

Outside of client work, I keep a small set of internal tools I've built myself — dashboards, tracking scripts, Shopify automations — because being able to build the tool you need, rather than waiting on someone else to, is often the fastest way to get an answer.
