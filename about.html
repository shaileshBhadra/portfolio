About

Professional profile.

What I do

Growth through search, data and systems.

I plan and execute SEO, analytics and paid media strategy for e-commerce and service businesses — from technical audits and GA4/GTM implementation to Shopify optimization and campaign management. I also build the small tools and automations that keep that work measurable.

How I work

Understand before I touch a tool.

Every engagement starts with the business objective, not the tactic. I audit the current state, form a hypothesis from the data, plan the highest-impact changes, implement them, and measure against the original objective — see my work pattern for the full loop.

My approach to AI

A force multiplier, not a replacement.

I use AI to accelerate research, coding, debugging and content drafting. It doesn't make the final call — every AI-assisted output is validated against real data (GSC, GA4, SEMrush) or tested before it ships. See the full breakdown on the AI & Automation page.

My technical approach

Enough code to solve the problem.

I'm not a full-time developer — I use HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python and Flask to build the scripts, dashboards and Shopify theme fixes that a marketing or SEO problem actually needs, and I know when a problem calls for an engineer instead.

Learning philosophy

Search and AI both move fast.

Algorithms, ad platforms and AI tooling all change quickly. I keep a standing practice of testing new tools, tracking platform updates, and re-validating what "best practice" means every few months rather than relying on what worked last year.

Current focus

AEO and server-side measurement.

Right now I'm focused on Answer Engine Optimization (AEO/GEO) as AI search surfaces grow, and on server-side GTM implementations that keep first-party tracking accurate as privacy constraints tighten.
