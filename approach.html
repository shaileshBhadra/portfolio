MY APPROACH

How I Approach SEO, Analytics & Performance Growth

Start with the business problem, not the tool.

Before opening an SEO crawler, building a dashboard or changing a campaign, I want to understand what the business is trying to achieve.

Because more traffic is not always the answer.
More rankings are not always the answer.
More data is definitely not always the answer.

The right answer starts with understanding where growth is being constrained.

SEO is the discipline.
Growth is the objective.
Measurement is how I know whether it worked.

01 — Understand the Business
02 — Diagnose the Current State

Find what is actually limiting performance.

The objective is not to produce the longest audit.
It is to identify the problems that matter.

03 — Prioritize the Opportunities

Not every issue deserves the same amount of time.

Potential Impact × Effort × Confidence

A focused roadmap is more valuable than a spreadsheet containing 200 equally important recommendations.

04 — Build the Strategy

The strategy connects the findings to the business goal.

    The strategy should tell the team what to do, why it matters and how success will be measured.

    05 — Move from Recommendations to Execution

    Recommendations only create value when somebody implements them.

    I work with the teams responsible for execution — including developers, content teams, designers, paid media specialists and marketing stakeholders — to turn strategy into action.

    Where appropriate, I can work directly on implementation rather than stopping at the recommendation.

    06 — Measure What Changed

    The metric depends on the business.

    SEO and digital acquisition are not one-time projects.

    After implementation, I look at what changed, what worked, what didn't and where the next opportunity exists.

    Diagnose → Prioritize → Execute → Measure → Learn → Improve
