How I work

How I run SEO and paid media work, step by step

This page explains the 7 steps I follow on every account. Each step ends with a real example from my own work and a link to the full case study.

Before the steps

One symptom can have several causes

A drop in rankings, low conversions or mismatched data each have more than one possible cause. Click a card to see what I check first.

Step 1 of 7 · Understand the business

Learn what the business needs before touching anything

Step 2 of 7 · Diagnose

Find what is actually limiting performance

The objective is not to produce the longest audit.
It is to identify the problems that matter.

Step 3 of 7 · Prioritize

Not every issue deserves the same amount of time

Potential Impact × Effort × Confidence

A focused roadmap is more valuable than a spreadsheet containing 200 equally important recommendations.

Step 4 of 7 · Build the strategy

Turn the findings into a plan the team can follow

The plan links each finding to the business goal.

    From Findings to Roadmap

    The strategy should tell the team what to do, why it matters and how success will be measured.

    Step 5 of 7 · Execute

    A recommendation only helps once someone implements it

    I work with the teams responsible for execution — including developers, content teams, designers, paid media specialists and marketing stakeholders — to turn strategy into action.

    Where appropriate, I can work directly on implementation rather than stopping at the recommendation.

    Step 6 of 7 · Measure

    Check what changed against the original goal

    The metric depends on the business.

    Step 7 of 7 · Improve

    Use the results to choose the next priority

    SEO is not audit → report → done.
    Diagnose → Execute → Measure → Learn → Improve.
