The Problems I Solve

Same symptoms, different root causes.

The same symptoms can come from very different root causes. I focus on finding the underlying problem before recommending a solution.

Rankings are a signal. Growth is the goal.

A ranking increase isn't automatically a business win. I look beyond rankings to understand whether organic visibility is producing qualified traffic, leads, revenue or another meaningful business outcome.

SEO works best when it is connected to measurement, conversion behaviour and business priorities.

Prioritisation

I don't optimise everything. I optimise what matters.

Large SEO audits can produce hundreds of recommendations. The job is not to fix everything at once. The job is to identify what can create the greatest business impact.
